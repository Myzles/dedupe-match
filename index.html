Clean up duplicate rows in Google Sheets, even when they don't match exactly

"Smith, John" and "John Smith". "ACME, Inc" and "Acme Inc.". "(555) 123-4567" and "555.123.4567". Dedupe & Match sees they are the same and shows you why, before anything changes.

Exact and close duplicates

Matches people, companies, emails, phones, websites and addresses the way a person would: capitals, spacing, punctuation, nicknames, name order and company endings all handled.

Review first, then act

Every match comes with a score and a reason. Then highlight, mark in a column, copy unique rows, merge duplicates into one row or remove them.

Compare two tabs

Last month's export against this month's: what's new, what's gone and exactly which values changed, in one tab.

Fuzzy lookup

Pull columns from another tab by closest match, or use the =FUZZYLOOKUP and =FUZZYSCORE formulas right in your cells.

Built for big lists

Private by design
