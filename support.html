Help and support

Getting started

  1. Open your spreadsheet and choose Extensions, then Dedupe & Match, then Open Dedupe & Match.
  2. Pick the tab. The columns that identify a row (name, company, email, phone, website) are ticked for you.
  3. Click Find duplicates. A new tab lists each group: the row kept, its duplicates, a match score and the reason.
  4. Happy with it? Click Highlight, Mark in a column, Copy unique rows, Merge duplicates or Remove duplicates.

How close is a match?

  • Exact: the same after ignoring capitals and extra spaces.
  • Strict: small typos and formatting differences.
  • Balanced (recommended): typos, nicknames, reordered names and company endings.
  • Loose: catches more look-alikes. Check the results before removing anything.

Compare two tabs

Choose both tabs and the column that identifies a row in each (email, ID or name). The result tab lists rows only in the first tab, rows only in the second, and rows where other columns changed, with the old and new values.

Lookup and formulas

The Lookup tab adds columns from another tab by closest match. In cells you can use =FUZZYLOOKUP(value, range, return_column, [min_match]) and =FUZZYSCORE(text1, text2).

Licenses

After you buy Pro, your key arrives by email. Open the sidebar, paste it under Plan and click Activate. To move it to another computer or account, just paste it there too. Lost your key? It's in your Polar receipt, or email us.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my license key?

It's in the receipt email from Polar, sent right after checkout. Search your inbox for "Dedupe & Match". You can also find it any time in your Polar customer portal, linked from that email.

The sidebar says my key is not valid.

Copy the whole key from the email, with no spaces before or after. If your subscription was cancelled or a payment failed, the key stops working at the end of the paid period. Renewing turns it back on.

Can I use one key on several computers or Google accounts?

Yes. The key is yours, not tied to a Google account. Paste it into the sidebar wherever you use the add-on. One key is for one person, so please don't share it.

What does "500 rows per run" mean?

The free version works on tabs or selections up to 500 rows each time you run it, with every feature included. For a bigger tab, either select up to 500 rows (tick "Only the selected cells") or upgrade to Pro for no limit.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your Polar receipt email. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of your first payment, no questions asked. Reply to your receipt email or write to us.

Will it delete my data?

Only if you click Remove duplicates, and it asks you to confirm first. Find duplicates, Copy unique rows and Merge duplicates write to new tabs and leave your data as it is. Anything that changes your tab can be undone with Edit, then Undo.

Why did it match (or not match) two rows?

Every match shows a score and a reason in the results tab. To catch more look-alikes choose Loose; to be more careful choose Strict or Exact. Untick columns that shouldn't count, such as notes or dates.

It says it stopped at a percentage and is continuing.

That's normal on very large sheets. Google limits each run to 6 minutes, so the add-on saves its progress and carries on by itself. Keep the sidebar open until it finishes.

Does my data go anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside Google Sheets™. Only your license key is sent to Polar to confirm it's valid. See the privacy policy.
