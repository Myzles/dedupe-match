Contact details for any business, in one click

Contact Finder reads a company's website and gives you its emails, phone numbers, social profiles, address and contact page. Free browser extension for Microsoft Edge and Firefox.

The right email first

Addresses on the company's own domain come first. Image names, tracking codes and placeholders are filtered out.

More than one page

It also checks the contact, about, team and imprint pages, and decodes emails written as "name [at] company [dot] com".

Private

No account and no tracking. Everything runs in your browser.

Bulk mode

Paste up to 5,000 websites. Duplicates are removed, progress is shown, and results export to CSV or Excel.
