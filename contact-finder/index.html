Straight answers

What it finds

Contact details a business has published on its own website. Each email and phone shows which page it was found on, so you can check it yourself.

What it does not do

It does not verify that an address is deliverable, guess addresses from names, or look up people. If a site publishes only a contact form, you get the form link instead.

How often it finds an email

In our test of 280 real business sites, an email was published and found on about two thirds of them. About a third publish none.
