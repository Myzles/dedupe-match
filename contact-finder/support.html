Help

Popup

Open a company website and click the Contact Finder button. It shows emails (company-domain addresses first), phone numbers, social profiles, the address and the contact page. Results are kept for 24 hours; use "Scan again" to refresh.

Bulk mode

Click "Bulk mode" in the popup. Paste websites one per line and click "Find contacts". The first time, the browser asks permission to read websites. Export the results as CSV or Excel.

Plans

Free: 10 lookups a day, bulk runs up to 10 websites. Pro: unlimited lookups and bulk runs up to 5,000 websites, $7/month or $70/year. Paste your license key on the Bulk mode page under "Your plan".

Why are some sites empty?

Some businesses publish no email or phone at all, or only a contact form. Contact Finder reports that instead of guessing.
