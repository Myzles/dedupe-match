Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Contact Finder runs entirely in your browser. There is no Contact Finder server, account, analytics or tracking.

What the extension reads

  • Popup: when you click the toolbar button, it reads the page in the current tab and a few pages of the same website (contact, about, team, imprint) to find published contact details.
  • Bulk mode: it reads the homepage and contact-related pages of the websites you paste, only after you grant permission.

Requests are made without your cookies. Nothing you browse is collected or sent anywhere.

What is stored

Results of recent lookups (so reopening the popup is instant), your last bulk run, your settings, a daily lookup counter and your license key are stored locally in the extension's storage on your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Network requests to third parties

Only one: when you activate Pro, and about once a day after that, your license key is sent to Polar (polar.sh), our payment provider, to confirm it is valid. No browsing data is included.

Permissions

  • activeTab + scripting: read the current tab when you click the button.
  • storage: keep results, settings and your license locally.
  • Access to websites (optional): requested only when you first use bulk mode, to read the websites you list. You can revoke it any time.

Your responsibility

Use the contact details you find in line with the law where you live (for example GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL) and the websites' terms.
