Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Dedupe & Match is a Google Sheets add-on made by Myzles ("we"). This policy explains what the add-on can access, what it does with it, and what it never does.

What the add-on accesses

  • The spreadsheet you open it in. The add-on asks only for permission to view and manage the spreadsheet it is used in (spreadsheets.currentonly). It cannot see your other spreadsheets, your Drive, your email or your contacts.
  • Showing its sidebar and dialogs inside Google Sheets (script.container.ui).
  • Connecting to an outside service (script.external_request), used only to check a license key with our payment provider, Polar.

How your data is used

All matching runs inside Google Apps Script, on Google's servers, under your own Google account. Spreadsheet contents are read only to find duplicates, compare tabs or look up matches, and results are written back to your spreadsheet. We never receive, store, sell or share your spreadsheet data. Nothing from your sheet is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your sidebar settings (which columns you chose, how strict matching should be) are saved in the spreadsheet's own add-on storage so they are there next time. If you enter a license key, it is saved in your Google account's add-on storage and sent to Polar (polar.sh) to confirm it is valid. No other information goes with it.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Polar Software Inc. as merchant of record. Polar collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy (polar.sh/legal/privacy). We receive your email address and order details from Polar so we can provide support.

Google user data

Dedupe & Match's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and no person reads it.

Cookies and tracking

The add-on uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. This website uses none either.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the add-on from Google Sheets (Extensions, then Add-ons, then Manage add-ons) removes its access. Result tabs it created are ordinary tabs you can delete. To have us delete your order records, email us at the address below.
